How does the free first video work?

Message me on WhatsApp and tell me about your business. We shoot one video together — you see the real quality and style before paying a cent. If you love it, we pick a package. If not, you keep the video anyway.

I hate being on camera. Is that a problem?

No. Most of my clients felt the same. I direct everything and we can build videos around your work, your space and your results — your face is optional, your business is the star.

What do you need from me?

About 2 hours once a month and access to your location. Ideas, scripts, shooting, editing, captions and post texts are all on me.

When can we start?

Onboarding calls and content planning start right away. Message me on WhatsApp and we'll set up your first shoot.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. Stay because it works, not because you're locked in.
